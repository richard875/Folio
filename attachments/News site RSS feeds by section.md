# Homepage RSS feeds, one per site

Oct 12, 2026 · @Richard Everley

FT's feed is a true copy of its homepage, NYT's and CNBC's are editor-ordered top stories. Most of the others are newest-first "latest" feeds. I opened each feed on 11–12 October 2026 and compared it with the live homepage where the site let me; NYT, The Economist, CNBC and The Washington Post block my tools, so their rows come from copies of the feeds you pasted.

- **Score:** how well the feed stands in for the homepage, judged against your screen captures of 12 Oct. 10 = the homepage's stories in page order; points off for missing lead stories, no importance order, clean-up needed, or not being RSS.
- **Formats:** all RSS 2.0 except Bloomberg, whose sitemaps are Google News sitemap XML (`<url>` entries instead of `<item>`).
- **Polling:** every source holds at least a day of stories except FT, which is a 12-story snapshot of its homepage, so hourly polling is plenty.
