# Homepage RSS feeds, one per site

Oct 12, 2026 · @Richard Everley

FT's feed is a true copy of its homepage, NYT's and CNBC's are editor-ordered top stories, and Axios's comes close. Most of the others are newest-first "latest" feeds, WSJ's comes from its own Flipboard account, and Reuters' is a Google-ranked search. I opened each feed on 11–12 October 2026 and compared it with the live homepage where the site let me; NYT, The Economist, CNBC and The Washington Post block my tools, so their rows come from copies of the feeds you pasted.

- **Score:** how well the feed stands in for the homepage, judged against your screen captures of 12 Oct. 10 = the homepage's stories in page order (Axios); points off for missing lead stories, no importance order, clean-up needed, or not being RSS.
- **Formats:** all RSS 2.0 except Bloomberg and Business Insider, whose sitemaps are Google News sitemap XML (`<url>` entries instead of `<item>`).
- **Polling:** every source holds at least a day of stories except FT, which is a 12-story snapshot of its homepage, so hourly polling is plenty.
- **WSJ clean-up:** strip `?mod=Flipboard` from links before deduping or matching. Opinion pieces are rarely posted there; add `feeds.content.dowjones.io/public/rss/RSSOpinion` if you want them.
